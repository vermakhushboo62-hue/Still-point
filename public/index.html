    Find what's keeping you stuck

    Go slowly. There are no wrong answers, and everything you write stays on this device.

    1. 1

      What's the stuck point?

      The thought that keeps looping. Pick the closest one, or write your own.

    2. 2

      The core belief underneath

      A deeper rule about yourself, other people or the world. Change the words until they feel true for you.

    3. 3

      Where might it come from?

      • When do you first remember feeling this way?
      • Whose voice does this belief sound like?
      • What did it protect you from back then?

      These are only possibilities. You know your story best. If this brings up something painful, it's okay to stop here, and a counsellor can help with deeper roots.

    4. 4

      A kinder, truer belief

      Something more balanced that you can still believe. Edit it until it sounds like you.

    5. 5

      Tiny efforts to grow it

      Small actions that prove the new belief a little at a time. Tick them off as you try them.
